Eyes Studio desktop: third-party notices

THIRD-PARTY-COMPONENTS.txt, beside this file, lists every library on the
studio-desktop runtime classpath and every bundled file, with its version,
licence (an SPDX expression) and copyright holder. The sbt build generates it
from the resolved dependency graph and the reviewed licence table
studio/desktop/licences.tsv (ticket S1.14); the build fails when a resolved
library has no entry (checkStudioNotices). components.tsv is the same list
for the About box.

licences/<SPDX id>.txt holds the text of every licence the list names
(licences/SOURCES.md gives their source and checksums).

Libraries that ship their own LICENSE and NOTICE files in their jar's
META-INF have them here too, as <artifact>-<version>-LICENSE.txt and
-NOTICE.txt: Apache PDFBox, PDFBox IO, FontBox and Commons Logging, which
figure export (S9.3) uses through Intaglio's PDF renderer. PDFBox's LICENSE
also carries the licences of the third-party material it contains.

The fonts embedded in exported figures are under the SIL Open Font License
1.1 (../fonts/OFL-*.txt), which permits embedding them in documents.
