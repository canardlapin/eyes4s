# S3.7 · Swap to the real eyes4s backend: slice plan

Ticket `bd-01M3DPFHY9YW5BPSJQ8VBBXHVN`. Base: main `cdc25580`, protocol 1.8. Protocol 1.9
(`pairRows`) is on `p0-pairrows`.

S3.7 implements `StudyBackend[F]` over eyes4s. Every acceptance criterion closed on
`FakeStudyBackend` must also pass on the real backend. This plan splits that work into slices of
at most half a day. Each slice lands and closes on its own, under `bd-01M3DPFHY9YW5BPSJQ8VBBXHVN`.

## Rules every slice keeps

- **No science in studio.** The real backend calls the library and converts its values into the
  studio protocol types. It never re-derives a count, score, placement or density. Summing
  durations the library has already tallied, or counting rows of a library ledger, is bookkeeping.
  Anything that decides what a fixation, trial or pair *is* stays in eyes4s.
- **Failures are typed values.** Every eyes4s refusal becomes a `BackendError` that names its
  operands: either an existing case, or a new case added in the slice, with its stable code and a
  protocol bump when it changes the wire. An effect fails only on a defect of the backend itself.
- **R is never a dependency.** Scores the real backend serves are compared with
  `fixtures/studio-golden/SCORES.json` (S0.7b), never with an R run.
- **Placement.** `RealStudyBackend` lives in `studio-core` shared sources (JVM and Scala.js).
  `studio-core` gains a compile dependency on `eyes4s-io`, which is cross-built and allowed to use
  effects; today `studio-core` reaches io only from its JVM tests.
  - The backend reads no files. Its datasets arrive as `DatasetRevisionSpec`s from the studio
    document, and its source contents arrive through a host port that the host provides:
    studio-desktop reads files, and JVM tests serve `fixtures/studio-golden`. The port was planned
    as `SourceContents[F]` and is named `DatasetSources[F]` in code.
  - `studio-app` stays pure. It never calls the backend's library code directly; it only receives
    `StudyBackend` responses.
- **Conformance.** `BackendConformanceSuite` is reused unchanged. The real-backend subclass
  (`RealBackendConformanceSuite`, a JVM test that reads the golden files) runs the subset of tests
  its slices support. That subset is an explicit list that grows by slice, so a test is never
  silently skipped. The fake keeps running every test.

## Slices

1. **Skeleton, admission and ledger (now).**
   - `RealStudyBackend[F]` over registered `DatasetRevisionSpec`s, `AnalysisRevisionSpec`s and a
     `SourceContents[F]` port.
   - `admission` and `ledger` call eyes4s-io under the revision's recorded mapping, units,
     geometry and admission choice.
   - The image-window tallies use `WindowTally.window`. Images found and missing are computed
     against the host's stimulus listing.
   - Every operation not yet implemented answers a typed refusal (`Unavailable` naming the
     revision, run or job). It never fakes data.
   - Unknown dataset, revision, run and job refusals match the fake's codes.
   - `RealBackendConformanceSuite` runs three tests: "admission reports the FIXTURE.md inventory
     and window totals", "the ledger pages through every inventory trial once and agrees with
     admission", and the dataset/revision half of "refusals are values…".
   - Inventory refusals become `BackendError.InventoryRefused` through `InventoryIssue.of`.
2. **Revision → `StudyPlan` and the static preview.** Build the plan from `AnalysisRevisionSpec`,
   refuse a recipe the bound plan disagrees with, and serve `preview` / `previewRows` from
   `StudyPreview`. Adds the conformance test "the preview's counts agree with its rows". Blocked
   on the preview-digest protocol change unless the stamp gains it in this slice (needs the lead's
   call on the protocol owner).
3. **Preview paging and submitting a ready preview** (needs 1.9 `pairRows`).
4. **Execution and progress over eyes4s-fs2.** Adds the subscription and cancellation
   conformance tests; `finish` waits for the real job.
5. **Results, queries and inspection.** Adds the result and inspection conformance tests; scores
   are checked against SCORES.json once S0.7b freezes it.
6. **Provenance** (after UI-G G3 and the G1 record retype).
7. **Trial views and source records** (after the core placement bead).
8. **Density grids for Figures panel C** (protocol minor bump plus a `StudyBackend` method).
9. **Descriptors** (after CR6), the full conformance and the E2E-01 / adversarial runs on the
   real backend, and the PR list re-checking every fake-closed ticket's real-backend acceptance

## Slice 1 record

`RealStudyBackend` and `RealAdmission` (studio-core `eyes4s.studio.core.real`) and the
`DatasetSources[F]` host port are in place. `RealBackendConformanceSuite` (JVM, over
fixtures/studio-golden at story moment t2) runs the three tests slice 1 serves:

- admission;
- the ledger;
- refusals.

`RealStudyBackendSuite` checks the parts that conformance does not cover:

- the ledger equals the fake's on all 960 trials;
- a page reports its own offset, size and next page;
- the typed refusals.

Findings for later slices and for the re-check list:

- **The overlap clock is named differently.** The fake's ledger matches eyes4s's on every trial
  except the operands of the six overlap quarantines. eyes4s names a trial's clock
  `fixation-trial:<key digest>`; the fake's generated ledger names it
  `fixation-trial:P03/Encoding/enc_11#1`. Studio shows this text in the overlap message. The fake
  should adopt eyes4s's form, or the generator should state it.
- **Not yet admitted, refused as `Unavailable(Dataset)`.** These are typed, but name no operand
  beyond the dataset:
  - a revision without declared time units;
  - a revision without an inventory (`InventoryJoin.Undeclared`);
  - a revision with coordinate corrections;
  - a fixation-table refusal other than the inventory's;
  - host bytes whose SHA-256 differs from the revision's record.

  A typed `AdmissionRefused` case naming the eyes4s error waits for the next protocol minor
  version, so that it does not collide with 1.9.
- **Images are counted from the host's registry, not eyes4s.** The images found and missing come
  from the host's stimulus registry (`AssetRegistry`, S2.10). The window's microsecond totals sum
  the durations of `WindowTally`; `WindowSummary` carries no durations. If the library should own
  that sum, it is a small addition to `WindowSummary`.

## Slice 1 review fixes (REVISE, 2026-10-04)

1. **Digest and admission refusals.** Until 1.11, a digest mismatch is `Unavailable` whose locus
   names the file and both digests. A missing field names the field, and an eyes4s refusal keeps
   eyes4s's message beside its source. Two ignored `PENDING 1.11` tests in
   `RealStudyBackendSuite` assert the typed `SourceDigestMismatch` and `AdmissionRefused`.
2. **Outside-frame records.** They are keyed by the full trial identity, occurrence included. A
   synthetic test keeps two trials' outside-screen records apart. Repeated occurrences of one
   trial label cannot merge either: eyes4s refuses such an inventory (`Conflict` on
   `occurrence`), and a test pins that.
3. **StudioLint rule 6.** Portable studio sources (core, app, viz, tests included) may not name
   the file-backed eyes4s-io objects `ArtifactFiles`, `ArrowResultExport`,
   `EyeLinkAscStreaming` or `EyeLinkAscImport`. The rule has a self-test and is wired into
   `checkStudioBoundaries`.
4. **Totals come from eyes4s.**
   - `WindowSummary` gains the duration totals (a library change to eyes4s-plan, which affects
     the api-audit). The backend uses `WindowSummary.of(tallies, ledger)` with the ledger from
     `FixationEvidence.ledger`.
   - Items are the stimulus registry's file count.
   - The admission summary equals the fake's entire summary (apart from the deprecated
     `history`), which kills the doubled-micros mutant.
5. **No sentinels or cached failures.** A trial with no item is refused rather than given `""`.
   A refusal is never cached; only a successful admission or preparation is.

## Slice 4 record (execution and progress, 2026-10-04)

`submit`, `jobs`, `job`, `subscribe`, `cancel`, `outcome` and `runs` run a revision's prepared
study on eyes4s-fs2's runner (`StudyExecution.submissionWithId`, `Execution.start`).

- **Ids.** The eyes4s run id is the studio (job, run) pair. One job runs at a time; a second
  submission is `AlreadyRunning`, as on the fake.
- **Reports are eyes4s's (`RealExecution`).**
  - A report's meter is the UI-D `StageMeter` eyes4s commits with each step, unchanged.
  - Its run totals carry the latest map and pair meters against the exact totals of the
    revision's `StudyCounts`. A completed run reports those totals as done, because an exact
    total is the count a completed run did.
  - Counting steps have no protocol stage, so the job stays `Queued` through them.
- **Fibers.** One fiber folds the runner's coalesced progress into the job. A second fiber
  settles the job from the runner's authoritative outcome, then releases the run.
- **Cancellation** goes through the runner. `cancel` waits until the job has settled, so the
  status it returns is final.
- **Failure.**
  - A refused run carries eyes4s's diagnostic for the whole `StudyRunError`
    (`Diagnose[StudyRunError]`, family `study-run`: plan, meter and unexpected-completion
    codes). Corrected in slice 5: slice 4 diagnosed only the plan case.
  - Only host defects settle `Failed` with an `Unavailable(Run)` diagnostic whose message names
    the defect: an exception the run raised, or telemetry this mapping cannot read.
- **Runs.** The document's runs are seeded with their states:
  - the shown run is current;
  - another completed run is stale once a later dataset revision is admitted, and completed
    otherwise;
  - a run recorded as running had an earlier backend's job, so it is not listed and its
    number is not reused.

  These seeded states equal the fake's at t2.
- **Not yet served.** Completed results are kept for slice 5. Until then, `result`, `queries`,
  `inspect` and `provenance` of a known run are `Unavailable(Run)`.
- **Conformance.** The real suite now serves the subscription and cancellation tests, on t2
  with the trial-inventory layout (slice 2 answer 1). `finish` drains the job's subscription.
- **Mutants killed:**
  - a cancelled run recorded as completed;
  - pair meters not carried into the run totals;
  - `cancel` returning before the job settles.

## Slice 5a record: recomputing document runs (2026-10-04)

The integration base was `p0-s37-s5`: `p0-s37` with `p0-unmatched` and `p0-report-digest` merged.
It was rebased onto main 09e6255a (batch 6, which carries the streaming digest) plus
`p0-unmatched`.

- **Recomputation (option (a), lead-approved with conditions).** A completed run of the document
  that this backend did not compute is recomputed on first request for its result.
  - Its revision's prepared study runs as an ordinary job: listed by `jobs`, followed by
    `subscribe`, cancelled by `cancel`. The run's own state is unchanged.
  - Until the job completes, the run is `Unavailable(Run)`. The typed form is a 1.11 candidate.
  - The result is kept per run, so a later request starts no job.
  - The result is marked `Recomputed(eyes4s release)` (`StudioBuild.eyes4sBaseVersion`), so it
    never passes for the original computation. Provenance shows it in slice 6.
- **Digest check.** When the run's archive binding is bound, the recomputed result's digest
  (`StudyResultCodecs.trialRegistered(method).codec.digest`) must equal it.
  - Otherwise the job fails, and the run is refused for good with `Unavailable(Artifact(...))`
    naming both digests. The typed form is a 1.11 candidate.
  - Open: whether `ResultArchiveArtifact` names this digest or that of the codec's
    `StudyResultArchive`. Studio has never bound one.
- **Other runs.**
  - A cancelled or failed run, or one this backend computed that did not complete, is `NoResult`
    with its state.
  - A run whose revision cannot be prepared returns the preparation's refusal (run 5: r2
    declares no time units).
- **Kept for the result views.** The prepared study keeps the method, its eyes4s codecs and the
  eyes4s `AdmissionLedger`, which `ReportSources.study` needs.
- **Mutants killed:**
  - no digest check;
  - recomputation changing the run's state;
  - recomputed result not kept.

## Slice 5b record: pair rows and inspection (2026-10-04)

**Lead decisions (on the S3.7 bead).**
- `QueryRow.matched` becomes `Option[TrialKey]` in the S3.7 protocol minor. It carries eyes4s's
  unmatched reason or the not-admitted disposition, never a re-derived match. Until then, `queries`
  is refused and its agreement test stays out of Served.
- Scale-dependent and grouped scalars leave `ResultSummary`. They will come from lane-s26's
  `report(run, spec, scale)` instead (bd-01M44P3SF52WPXQXFXSMXC5R8B, Compare lane). Until then,
  the real backend serves no `ResultSummary`.

**What 5b serves.** `pairRows` (protocol 1.9) and `inspect` read the held result through eyes4s
`ResultInspection.study(plan, result, input, ledger)`, built once per run (`RealResults`).
- **Pair rows.** Rows come in focal order, each query's matched pairs before its controls. Each
  row carries eyes4s's score or its diagnostic. An unknown scale is `UnknownScale`.
- **Inspect.** It serves contrast rows (M, B and D from the row's two reductions), reductions (value
  and selected pairs) and pairs (reference item and score).
  - A failed item is `Unscored(Failed(eyes4s diagnostic))`.
  - Estimation is `Unavailable` (MapGridOf, lane-s56).
  - A no-match query's contrast row is eyes4s's failed row (`contrast-row.missing-operands`) until
    1.11 reports NoMatch with eyes4s's reason.
  - A not-admitted query has no item.

**Checked against SCORES.json (S0.7b, embedded as `GoldenScores`).** Run 7, recomputed, matches it
to 6 decimals:
- every contributing query's contrast (M, B, D) at all four scales;
- its matched pair score at every scale;
- its matched and control reductions, and the matched pair, at 2°;
- 8,969 pair rows per scale;
- each failed query's eyes4s code.

**Mutants killed:** controls ordered before matched pairs; M and B swapped; reduction members off by
one.

- **7a: `trialFixations`.**

    per revision: its source record, screen centre, interval and `MapPlacement`.
  - An inventory trial without an admitted scanpath is `Unavailable(Trial)`. A trial outside the

  - All 937 admitted golden trials equal the fake's views.

  - eyes4s-io `FixationSourceText` checks the file against the ledger's source digest. It pages

  - A record's cells are read as numbers, with times converted from the declared unit to ms.
  - Its image position and degrees are the kernel's (`RecordPositions`, shared with the fake).
  - All 11,520 records, in 24 pages, equal the fake's.
- **7b: `trialPreview`** is waiting on the lead. eyes4s has no public single-trial estimate under a
  plan. The proposed addition is `PreparedStudy.estimate(key, estimate)`, returning a
  `DensityView`.
- **Mutants killed:**
  - onset scale;
  - a constant placement, in both views;
  - record number off by one;
  - line text changed;
  - time unit ignored.

## Slice 6 record: provenance (2026-10-04)

  matched is optional.
